Privacy Policy — Liftloq

Effective date: 11 September 2026 · Last updated: 11 September 2026
App: Liftloq (package com.konstantyp.gymcal)
Developer: konstantyp · Contact: p4we1.dev@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the Liftloq Android application (“the App”) handles information. Liftloq is a local workout calendar. It does not require an account and does not operate a Liftloq backend or cloud sync service.

1. Summary

  • Workout types, calendar assignments, settings, and related app data are stored only on your device.
  • We do not create user accounts or upload your workout plan to our servers (we do not run such servers).
  • The App may optionally fetch a motivation quote from a third party over the internet when that feature is enabled and a connection is available.
  • If you install Liftloq from Google Play, Google may process data under Google’s own policies.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally (for example via Android DataStore / app storage):

  • Custom workout types (names and colors)
  • Which workouts you assigned to which days (including up to two workouts per day)
  • App preferences (language, quote visibility, and similar settings)

This information stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it (see below). Uninstalling the App typically removes this local data, subject to Android backup settings you control.

3. Optional network use (motivation quotes)

If “Show motivation quote” is enabled, the App may request a random quote from zenquotes.io (https://zenquotes.io/api/random). That request is for app functionality (displaying a quote). The App does not send your workout plan or account credentials to ZenQuotes (there is no Liftloq account). If the request fails or you are offline, the quote area is simply hidden.

ZenQuotes’ handling of connection data is governed by that service’s own terms and privacy practices.

4. Export / share

You may export or import your workout plan as a JSON file, or share files using Android system share sheets. Those actions are user-initiated. We do not automatically upload exported files.

5. Permissions

The App declares the INTERNET permission for optional quote fetching. Widgets and calendar features work with local data. The App does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs from the developer.

6. Children

Liftloq is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: email p4we1.dev@gmail.com or open an issue on github.com/konstantyp/Gymcal.

This page is provided for transparency for Google Play and users. It is not legal advice.
